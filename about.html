About Ravzen Ventures

The firm behind the corridor.

An international project-investment and capital advisory firm, built around one relationship per engagement and one route for capital to travel.

Our story

Ravzen Ventures LLP was founded on a simple observation: the best projects and the right capital are rarely in the same room, and the intermediaries meant to connect them usually add distance rather than remove it.

We built a firm that does the opposite. Every engagement runs through a single advisor who carries the full context of both the project and the investor — from the first conversation to the closing wire. No panels, no hand-offs, no diluted accountability.

Today we work the corridor between India, the UAE, Singapore and the United States, matching promoters who need capital with the funds, family offices, lenders and strategic investors who deploy it.

Why this corridor

India originates a disproportionate share of investable projects. The UAE, Singapore and the US hold a disproportionate share of the capital looking for them. The distance between the two is where value is lost — and where we work.

Each market plays a distinct role: India for origination and on-ground diligence, the UAE for private and family capital, Singapore for funds and holding structures, and the US for institutional and strategic investors. Knowing how they connect is the advisory.

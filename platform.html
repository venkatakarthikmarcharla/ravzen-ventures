What it does

First built for Mumbai's society redevelopment market, the Ontology is a data integration layer that aggregates, structures and monitors the information that determines whether a development project is fundable, executable and investable — in real time.

It brings together landowner consents, legal title, regulatory approvals, construction tracking, sales velocity and investor disbursements into a single, structured view — one that updates as the project moves.

Instead of chasing status updates across lawyers, architects, contractors and sales teams, you and your investors see the same live picture.

Who it's for

  • Developers managing multiple stakeholders and approvals
  • Promoters preparing for a fundraising mandate
  • Investors who need real-time project monitoring post-disbursement
  • Advisors and lenders who need structured diligence data

Current focus

The Ontology is currently in active use for society redevelopment projects in Mumbai. We are expanding to cover commercial, mixed-use and infrastructure projects across India and the UAE.

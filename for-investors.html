For Investors

Carefully assessed opportunities. Transparent information. No overclaiming.

We work with investors, family offices and strategic capital looking for relevant opportunities across India, the UAE, Singapore and the USA. We only bring opportunities we have assessed ourselves — and we are transparent about what we know and what we do not.

Our commitment to investors

What you can expect when you work with Ravzen.

01

Pre-assessed opportunities

We only introduce opportunities we have reviewed ourselves. We will tell you what we found — including the risks and gaps — not just the headline numbers.

02

Full information disclosure

You receive clear information about the project, the promoter and the structure. We do not selectively present information to make an opportunity look better than it is.

03

Relevant matching only

We introduce you only to projects that fit your stated mandate, sector preference and investment criteria. We do not send blanket deal flow and expect you to filter.

04

Conflict disclosure

Where we have an advisory relationship with the project promoter, we will disclose it. You always know our relationship with each party before you engage.

05

Cross-border structuring

We help navigate the legal, regulatory and banking requirements when capital and project sit in different jurisdictions — India, UAE, Singapore or the USA.

06

Confidentiality

Your investment criteria and interests are kept confidential. We will not share your name or involvement with any project promoter without your prior consent.

What we will not claim

Honest about what we are and what we are not.

  • We do not represent a global investor network with committed capital
  • We do not guarantee returns, valuations or deal outcomes
  • We do not imply endorsement by any investor we have worked with
  • We do not use investor names or logos without explicit permission
  • We do not introduce opportunities outside your stated criteria

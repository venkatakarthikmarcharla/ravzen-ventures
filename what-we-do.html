The mandate

Six services, delivered as one continuous engagement.

01

Capital raising

Structuring and running raises across equity, debt and hybrid instruments, sized and sequenced to the project's actual stage — not a template pitch deck.

02

Investor origination

Direct access to funds, family offices, private equity and venture capital investors, and strategic and institutional lenders across four markets.

03

Deal structuring

Term sheets, cap tables and financing instruments built to hold up under real due diligence, not just to look good in a first meeting.

04

Cross-border execution

Coordinating the legal, regulatory and banking steps that a raise touches when the investor and the project sit in different countries.

05

Investor relations

Ongoing reporting and communication once capital is deployed, so the relationship survives past the closing date.

06

Project vetting

We take on projects we've diligenced ourselves. Investors we introduce are meeting a project we'd put our own name behind.

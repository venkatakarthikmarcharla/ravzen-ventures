This Cookie Policy explains how Ravzen Ventures LLP (“Ravzen Ventures”) uses cookies and similar technologies on this website. It should be read together with our Privacy Policy.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help the site function, remember your preferences, and understand how the site is used.

2. Cookies we use

  • Essential cookies — required for the website to function, including remembering your cookie preferences. These cannot be switched off.
  • Analytics cookies — help us understand how visitors use the site so we can improve it. These are only set with your consent.

3. Managing your preferences

When you first visit, a banner lets you accept or decline non-essential cookies. You can change your choice at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser, which will make the banner appear again. Most browsers also let you block or delete cookies through their settings; disabling essential cookies may affect how the site works.

4. Third-party cookies

If we use third-party services (for example, website analytics), those providers may set their own cookies. Their use of cookies is governed by their own policies.

5. Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date above shows when it was last revised.
